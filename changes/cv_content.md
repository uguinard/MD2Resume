## Professional Profile

Holistic Language Educator with a Master's in ELE and a deep commitment to fostering human connection and natural language acquisition. My practice connects hearts and minds by grounding language in experiential learning, contextual induction, and 4E Cognition — moving away from rote textbooks toward living, physical interaction.

While my background spans structured international schools to remote Indigenous communities in the Amazonian watershed, my core strengths are my radical adaptability and constant curiosity. I am deeply open to the principles of self-directed exploration and agency. I value the natural ability to wonder, to learn alongside my students with a "beginner's mind," and above all, to build and belong to a resilient, restorative community.
